Why does this happen? Explore the science

Across a period, effective nuclear attraction generally increases. Down a group, additional shells and shielding generally reduce attraction.

About & Scientific Accuracy

This periodic table is an educational reference. Its animated atomic model is a simplified illustration of electron-shell distribution, not a depiction of real electron trajectories. Scientific measurements may depend on conditions, and some properties—especially for short-lived synthetic elements—are predicted, uncertain, or not yet measured. Not every element profile has been individually verified; consult the linked scientific references before relying on specific values. Atomic weights use CIAAW 2024 abridged values where available; bracketed whole numbers represent isotope mass numbers, not standard atomic weights. The 34 isotope mass numbers have been aligned with the CIAAW/NUBASE2020 reference table; some isotopes have multiple plausible selections.

Authoritative references: IUPAC · Royal Society of Chemistry · NIST
